Support

Questions, problems or feedback: email [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL]. We aim to reply within two working days.

Manage or cancel NOIR+

NOIR+ is billed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose NOIR. You can also reach it from NOIR under Profile → Settings → NOIR+.

Restore a purchase

Signed in on a new phone? In NOIR, open Profile → Settings → Restore purchases, using the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My reading won’t generate

Readings are written online, so check your connection and try again. If it keeps failing, email us with the approximate time it happened.

My rising sign looks wrong

Your rising sign depends on your exact birth time — it changes every couple of hours — and on choosing the right birthplace from the search. You can see both in your profile — if either is wrong, email us and we will correct it.

Delete your account

In NOIR, go to Profile → Settings → Delete account. This permanently erases your profile, readings and journal. It does not cancel NOIR+, which Apple bills separately.
