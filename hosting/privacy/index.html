Privacy Policy

Effective 7 October 2026

NOIR (“we”, “us”) is operated by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY]. This policy explains what NOIR collects, why, who processes it, and how to delete it. If you have a question, contact us at [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL].

The short version

  • We collect what NOIR needs to write your reading: your answers, your birth details and your account.
  • To write readings, your answers and your calculated chart positions are sent to Google’s Gemini AI — only after you agree to it in the app.
  • No advertising, no tracking, no analytics, and we never sell your data.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app, at any time.

What we collect

Account
Your email address, and your name if Apple or Google provides it when you sign in. We record which sign-in method you used. If you use email, your password is handled by Firebase Authentication and is never visible to us.
Your answers
Your answers to NOIR’s five onboarding questions.
Birth details
Your date and time of birth, and your birthplace — the city name, its coordinates and its time zone. These are used to calculate your natal chart on your device.
Readings and journal
The readings NOIR writes for you, your daily readings, and anything you write in your journal.
Subscription
Whether NOIR+ is active, which plan, and when it renews or ends, plus Apple’s transaction reference. Payment is handled entirely by Apple — we never receive your card or payment details.
Preferences
Whether the daily reminder is on and what time it is set for.

How your birthplace is found

Birthplace search runs entirely on your device using a built-in list of cities. What you type into the search is not sent to any mapping or location service. NOIR never asks for, or uses, your current location.

AI processing

NOIR uses Google’s Gemini AI, through Firebase AI Logic, to write your readings. We ask your permission in the app before anything is sent. When a reading is written, Gemini receives:

  • your onboarding answers;
  • the planetary positions in your natal chart, which NOIR calculates from your birth details on your device;
  • for daily readings, that day’s planetary positions, the archetype and traits from your reading, and short summaries of your recent daily readings.

Your name, email address, and your raw date, time and place of birth are not sent. Your journal entries are never sent to the AI.

Readings are for entertainment and personal reflection. They are not predictions, and not medical, psychological, financial or legal advice.

Who processes your data

Google (Firebase)
Hosts your account (Firebase Authentication) and your data (Cloud Firestore), and runs the AI that writes readings (Firebase AI Logic / Gemini). Firebase App Check confirms requests come from the genuine NOIR app; it uses Apple’s device-attestation services and does not identify you.
Apple
Processes NOIR+ subscriptions and, if you use it, Sign in with Apple.
Google Sign-In
Used only if you choose to sign in with Google.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not share your data with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled on your device. No notification content is sent through our servers.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, answers, birth details, readings, journal and reminder settings are permanently deleted, and your login is removed. If you signed in with Apple, NOIR also asks Apple to disconnect NOIR from your Apple ID.

Deleting your account

In NOIR, go to Profile → Settings → Delete account. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. Deleting your account does not cancel a NOIR+ subscription, because Apple bills it — cancel it in your Apple ID settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your data, and to object to or restrict how it is processed. You can delete everything yourself in the app; for anything else, contact us at the address above and we will respond within 30 days.

Security

Your data is stored with Google Firebase. Access rules ensure only your signed-in account can read or change your data, and connections to NOIR’s services are encrypted.

Children

NOIR is not intended for anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
